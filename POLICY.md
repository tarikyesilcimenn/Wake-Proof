## Privacy Policy

WakeProof is an offline proof-based alarm app designed to help users wake up by completing proof missions such as QR, NFC, steps, math, shake, and wake check tasks.

WakeProof does not require an account or login.

### Information Stored on Your Device

WakeProof stores alarm and proof data locally on your device, including:

- Alarm times and labels
- Repeat days and alarm settings
- Proof mission settings
- Locally generated QR station secrets
- NFC station identifiers, when used
- Local wake-up history and streak statistics
- App settings

This information is stored on your device and is deleted when you delete the app, unless your device backup settings preserve app data.

### Information We Do Not Collect

WakeProof does not directly collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Account credentials
- Contacts
- Photos from your library
- Microphone recordings
- Location data
- Advertising identifiers

WakeProof does not include ads and does not require social login.

### Camera, Notifications, Motion, and NFC

WakeProof may request device permissions to provide app features:

- Notifications are used to alert you when an alarm or wake check is due.
- Camera access is used only for live QR proof missions.
- Motion or activity data may be used for step, movement, or shake proof missions.
- NFC access may be used for NFC proof stations on supported devices.

These permissions are used to operate WakeProof features. WakeProof does not upload your QR station secrets, NFC identifiers, alarm history, or local statistics to a WakeProof server.

### Purchases and RevenueCat

WakeProof uses RevenueCat to manage Premium purchase and subscription status.

RevenueCat may receive purchase-related information from the App Store, such as product identifiers, transaction status, subscription status, app user identifiers generated by the purchase SDK, device information, and diagnostic information needed to provide purchase functionality.

WakeProof uses this information only to determine whether Premium features are unlocked.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available here:

https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple handles App Store payments and may process information according to Apple's own policies.

### Log Data

If an error occurs, platform services or third-party purchase services may process technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, purchase status, time of request, and diagnostic logs. This information is used for reliability, debugging, and purchase support.

### Children's Privacy

WakeProof is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

### Security

WakeProof is designed to keep alarm and proof data on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures and rely on Apple platform protections and trusted third-party purchase infrastructure.

## Subscription and Auto-Renewal Policy

WakeProof may offer Premium subscriptions through the App Store.

Subscription details, duration, free trial availability, and pricing are shown in the app and on the App Store purchase sheet before you confirm a purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account may be charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings:

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone.
2. Tap your Apple ID name.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **WakeProof**.
5. Change or cancel the subscription.

Deleting the app does not automatically cancel a subscription.

If a free trial is offered, the trial terms are shown before purchase. Any unused portion of a free trial may be forfeited when a subscription is purchased, where applicable.
