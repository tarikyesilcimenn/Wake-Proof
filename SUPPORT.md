## Common Questions

### My alarm did not ring

Open **Setup** in WakeProof and check **Alarm Readiness**. Make sure notifications are allowed and that Focus, Silent Mode, and system volume are not preventing alerts from being heard.

WakeProof also explains iOS alarm reliability inside the app. iOS does not allow third-party apps to register the same kind of system alarm as Apple's Clock app, so WakeProof uses local notifications and in-app checks where available.

### I purchased Premium but it is still locked

Open the paywall and tap **Restore**. This asks the App Store and RevenueCat to refresh your purchase status.

If Premium is still locked, email support with the Apple ID region used for the purchase and the approximate purchase date. Do not send passwords or payment card information.

### How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not directly inside WakeProof.

On iPhone:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap your Apple ID name.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **WakeProof**.
5. Cancel or change the subscription.

### Is my alarm data uploaded?

WakeProof is designed to keep alarm and proof data on the device. Alarm settings, proof stations, QR secrets, and local stats are stored locally on your device.

RevenueCat is used only for purchase and subscription status.
